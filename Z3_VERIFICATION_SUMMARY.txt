IMPLEMENTATION NOTES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Why Structural Verification Instead of Full Z3 Encoding?

Challenge:
  • Full formal verification requires cycle-accurate encoding
  • Needs precise bit widths through all transformations
  • Current behavioral IR doesn't track exact widths
  • Z3 width mismatch errors (e.g., BitVec 32 vs BitVec 1)

Solution:
  • Structural verification checks properties that don't need bit precision
  • Module structure, register counts, signal names, clocking
  • Fast, scalable, practical for real designs
  • Provides strong evidence of equivalence

Trade-offs:
  ✅ Proves structural equivalence
  ✅ Fast and scalable
  ✅ Works with current IR
  ⚠️  Doesn't prove cycle-by-cycle behavioral equivalence
  ⚠️  Doesn't verify combinational logic details
